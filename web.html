About rates

Prices per gram are what you pay: investment gold is VAT exempt, other metals include VAT (on the full price, or on the dealer's margin only), and VAT is added for shops that quote without it. Metal kept in a vault can be VAT-free. See taxes by country.

Exchange rates

Prices are ranked in euros. Each price is converted at the exchange rate of the day of its scan, and the price per gram includes VAT as explained above. The Currency menu then converts all prices with the latest rates.

Rates are the European Central Bank's daily euro reference rates{{if .ECBCurrencies}} ({{range $i, $c := .ECBCurrencies}}{{if $i}}, {{end}}{{$c}}{{end}}){{end}}, published on working days around 16:00 CET.{{if .FallbackCurrencies}} For currencies the ECB does not publish ({{range $i, $c := .FallbackCurrencies}}{{if $i}}, {{end}}{{$c}}{{end}}), rates come from open.er-api.com, updated daily.{{end}}

About PM Scanner

What is it? A free, open-source price comparison for physical precious metals: 1 kg gold bars; gold coins such as the Krugerrand, Maple Leaf, Vienna Philharmonic, Britannia, American Eagle, Kangaroo, Sovereign, 20 Mark, Vreneli and Napoleon; silver bars and Maple Leafs; platinum, palladium and copper. Each product links straight to the dealer's page.

How are prices compared? Every price is converted to euros and divided by the product's weight, so a 1 oz coin, a 100 g bar and a 1 kg bar can be compared per gram. VAT is included as the buyer pays it, by country, as shown in the taxes table above.

How often is it updated? A scan runs every day; past scans stay available from the Scan menu, and every scan's prices can be downloaded as JSON.

Missing a dealer, or run one? The scraper and the list of shops are on GitHub; see For shop owners to add or remove a shop.
